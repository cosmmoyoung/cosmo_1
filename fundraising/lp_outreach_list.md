# Asia LP Outreach List — Fundraising Target List

*Prepared: 2026-07-22 · 33 Asia-Pacific targets · Draft for internal review. A Hong Kong–based, USD, fundamental public-markets manager.*

This is an **Asia-only, angle-driven** target list. It leads with the conversations already live, then works out by market. Every name is a **realistic source of USD capital we can access**, and each carries a specific reason we would be in the room — not a directory of everyone who is large.

## The criteria (rethought)

A name earns a place only if it clears all three:

1. **Asia-Pacific.** We have cut the Middle East, Europe and the Americas to keep this an Asia list. (The Abu Dhabi / Lunate side is *our own* backing, not a target.)
2. **A realistic source of USD capital for us.** That means it can put money into our USD fund — through a conventional external-manager mandate, a discretionary allocation, **or a live / strategic relationship**. We no longer exclude an institution just because it is not a "textbook" fund LP: if there is a real relationship and a plausible structure, it counts (this is why Silk Road Fund and the HKMA are in, at the top).
3. **A concrete angle.** A live conversation, a warm introduction, or a specific thesis / programme fit — not merely "it is big and allocates."

| **Status: Cold** | On-thesis, but needs cultivation / a process to enter. |
| **Tier 1 / 2 / 3** | Near-term priority within each status: first wave, cultivate, aspirational. |
