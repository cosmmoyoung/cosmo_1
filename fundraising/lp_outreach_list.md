*Prepared: 2026-07-22 · 284 institutions across 13 regions/categories · Draft for internal review. Written for an independent, Hong Kong–based fundamental public-markets manager.*

## 1. Purpose & scope

This is a structured universe of Limited Partners (LPs) and capital-gating intermediaries we could approach to anchor and build the fund. It is deliberately **global but Asia-weighted**, because that is where our edge, our network, and our warm relationships sit. Each institution carries a type, an approximate size, an assessment of whether it actually allocates to *external* managers, a suggested outreach tier, a short profile, and the reason we would reach out.

## 2. Working assumptions (please confirm or adjust)

These assumptions drive the **tiering** (they do not change which institutions exist), so correcting them lets us re-tier quickly:

- **Manager profile:** an independent / emerging manager based in Hong Kong.
- **Strategy:** a fundamental, research-driven public-markets / equities strategy (hedge fund or long-biased vehicle).
- **Ticket capacity:** able to absorb institutional cheques from the low tens of millions up to a few hundred million USD.
- **Stage:** raising an early institutional vehicle, so seeders, emerging-manager programmes, family offices, and hedge-fund-specialist gatekeepers matter disproportionately.

If any of these are wrong (e.g. a private-markets strategy, or a much larger/smaller target size), tell me and I will recalibrate the tiers.

## 3. Methodology & data caveats

- Built by parallel region-by-region research, with each institution's size, headquarters, type, and external-allocation behaviour web-verified against annual reports and reputable public sources (citations are in the Excel).
- **AUM figures are approximate and carry different as-of dates** (mostly 2023–2025). They are for prioritisation only. **Verify on Bloomberg / a terminal before any figure is quoted externally.**
- "Allocates externally" reflects whether the institution hires *external* managers / commingled funds (versus investing only in-house or directly) — a key filter for whether they can even be our LP.
- Duplicates across regions were merged; 45 entries are flagged **Low confidence** and should be re-checked before outreach (listed in §9).

## 4. How to read the tiers (outreach priority, **not** importance)

Tiers reflect **near-term outreach priority for a manager at our stage** — a blend of strategy fit, how actively the institution backs external / emerging managers, realistic accessibility, and relationship warmth. A mega-sovereign sitting in Tier 3 is not "less important"; it is simply harder to convert in the first wave.

| Tier | Meaning | What we do with it |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1 — First wave** | Strong fit, active external-/emerging-manager or hedge-fund programme, realistically reachable in ~6–12 months, and/or a warm path exists. | Concentrate outreach here now. |
| **Tier 2 — Cultivate** | Genuine external-allocation appetite but a longer cycle, a track-record / AUM threshold, or a consultant/placement gate. | Start relationship-building in parallel. |
| **Tier 3 — Aspirational** | Highly desirable but hardest to convert near-term — very large minimums, slow multi-year processes, strict emerging-manager rules, or mostly in-house. | Keep warm for later vintages. |

## 5. LP-type glossary

| Type | What it is |
|---|---|
| Sovereign Wealth Fund | State-owned investment fund (e.g. CIC, GIC, ADIA). |
| Public Pension Fund | Government-employee or national pension scheme (e.g. NPS Korea, CalPERS). |
| National Provident / Social Security Fund | Mandatory national savings/social-security pool (e.g. EPF Malaysia, NSSF China). |
| Mutual-Aid / Retirement Association | Profession-based retirement pools (common in Korea/Japan, e.g. POBA). |
| Insurance Company | Life/general insurers investing their float and policyholder reserves. |
| University Endowment | University long-term investment office (e.g. Yale, HMC). |
| Foundation / Charity / Endowed Trust | Philanthropic endowments (e.g. Wellcome Trust, Ford Foundation). |
| Fund-of-Funds / Multi-Manager | Pools capital and allocates across multiple external managers. |
| OCIO (Outsourced CIO) | Runs entire portfolios for institutions/families, selecting managers on their behalf. |
| Consultant / Investment Gatekeeper | Advises institutions on manager selection (e.g. Albourne, Mercer). |
| Private Bank / Wealth Management Platform | Aggregates HNW / family-office capital into funds (e.g. UBS, iCapital). |
| Single-Family Office | Manages one wealthy family's capital. |
| Multi-Family Office | Manages several families' capital, often allocating to external funds. |
| Seeder / GP-stakes / Acceleration Capital | Provides early anchor capital / working capital to new managers. |
| Development Finance Institution | State-backed development investor (usually mandate-constrained). |
| Central Bank / Monetary Authority (reserves) | Manages FX reserves; selectively uses external managers. |
