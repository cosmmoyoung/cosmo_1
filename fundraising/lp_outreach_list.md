# LP Outreach Shortlist — Fundraising Target List (focused)

*Prepared: 2026-07-22 · 49 high-conviction targets · Draft for internal review. A HK-based, USD, fundamental public-markets manager.*

This is the **focused, angle-driven** shortlist. Every name has passed three hard filters, and each carries a specific reason we would be in the room. The broader 284-name research universe is retained for reference in the Excel (`Full Universe` tab) and in `data/lp_universe.json`, but it is a long-list, not a call sheet.

## How this list is filtered

A name only earns a place if it clears **all three** tests:

1. **It actually invests in external funds as an LP** — not a direct-only investor, not an in-house-only balance sheet, and not a distributor that merely on-sells products to its own clients.
2. **It can write a USD ticket into an offshore fund** — so RMB-locked, QDII-constrained or domestic-only mandates are out.
3. **There is a real angle** — a warm path (Abu Dhabi/Lunate, CIC, Hong Kong) or a specific thesis reason it would back *us* — not merely "it is large and allocates."

## How to read the tiers (near-term outreach priority)

| Tier | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Tier 1 — First wave** | Real fund-LP behaviour **plus** a warm path or a purpose-built emerging-manager/seed appetite. Start here now. |
| **Tier 2 — Cultivate** | Genuine USD external-fund appetite and a real angle, but a longer, often consultant-gated cycle. |
| **Tier 3 — Aspirational** | On-thesis but hard to convert near-term (scale thresholds, slow procurement, or mostly in-house). |
